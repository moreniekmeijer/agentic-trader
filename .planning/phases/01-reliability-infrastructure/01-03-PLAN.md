## Context
- **Observed failure**: `GOOGL` entered trading after a failed/empty upstream fetch and crashed in `FeatureBuilder` with `single positional indexer is out-of-bounds`.
- **Observed failure**: `BRK-B` reached Alpaca order submission and failed with `asset "BRK-B" not found`.
- **Desired behavior**: The worker should survive partial upstream data failures and should skip or normalize symbols that are not directly tradable through Alpaca.

## Gap Closure Scope

### 1. Market Data Safety

#### [MODIFY] `agentic_trader/worker/worker.py`
- Refuse to cache market data for symbols whose daily or 4h DataFrames are empty or too short for feature construction.
- Keep bad symbols out of `state.symbols` so they never reach trading.
- Log a clear warning that the symbol was skipped because market data was incomplete.

#### [MODIFY] `agentic_trader/services/market_data/feature_builder.py`
- Add defensive validation before reading `iloc[-1]` and `iloc[-2]`.
- Raise a clear domain error or return a controlled failure when fewer than 2 rows are available.

### 2. Alpaca Symbol Compatibility Handling

#### [MODIFY] `agentic_trader/controller/alpaca_controller.py`
- Add symbol normalization and/or tradability validation before order submission.
- Normalize unsupported-asset responses into a recognizable failure path.

#### [MODIFY] `agentic_trader/worker/worker.py`
- Skip symbols that are not Alpaca-compatible under the current symbol format.
- Prevent unsupported symbols from producing noisy order submission exceptions.
- Log a clear warning when a scanned symbol is not tradable through Alpaca as-is.
